Contactless Sensing Principle

A single patch of aluminum foil is taped to the outside of the plastic overhead tank. ESP32 monitors capacitance via GPIO 4 Touch0. When water touches the inner wall, the dielectric shift cuts the pump instantly without any drilling or corrosion.
